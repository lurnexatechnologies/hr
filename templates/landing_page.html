Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kyro People & HR management activities.

Kyro People is a comprehensive Human Resource Management System (HRMS) designed to automate all HR management activities including self-onboarding, leave tracking, facial biometric attendance, automated payroll, and statutory PF/ESI compliance.

Kyro People is 100% statutory compliant. It automatically calculates Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT), and Income Tax deductions, generating instant digital payslips and Form-16 reports.

Kyro People uses 1:1 facial biometric descriptor vector matching against the employee's uploaded profile photo. Employees complete live face verification during WFH or clock-in, eliminating buddy punching completely.
